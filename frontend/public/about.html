Our Purpose & Heritage

Bridging Ancestral Artistry with Digital Possibility

BalochHunar was founded on a singular realization: traditional artisans create world-class masterpieces, but remain invisible to the global economy due to a lack of professional digital presence.

The Problem We Are Solving

  • Master artisans rely on informal channels (WhatsApp, word-of-mouth, middlemen) that capture most of the profit margin.
  • Lack of organized, high-resolution product catalogs and structured pricing.
  • Untold cultural stories — consumers buy generic souvenirs without knowing the generational legacy behind each piece.
  • Economic vulnerability among rural craftswomen and indigenous makers.

The BalochHunar Solution

  • Digital Storefront & Showcase: Professional, responsive catalog with live search, categorization, and inquiry channels.
  • Artisan Profile Pages: Elevating the maker, their location, experience, and craft lineage.
  • AI-Assisted Storytelling: Automated generation of compelling product descriptions and cultural narratives in the administrative panel.
  • Direct Custom Commissions: Structured booking for bespoke bridal embroidery, bulk corporate orders, and workshops.
Preserving Intangible Heritage

The Four Pillars of Baloch Craftsmanship

Balochi Doch Embroidery

Geometric micro-needlework with silk threads and embedded mirrors (Sheesha) created primarily in Turbat and Makran.

Vegetable-Tanned Leather

Generational leather carving and brass-studded chappals tanned with indigenous tree barks in Sibi.

Terracotta & River Clay

Porous earthenware jugs and glazed decorative pottery hand-thrown using alluvial riverbed silt from Panjgur.

Tribal Mountain Kilims

Flatweave geometric rugs hand-loomed from high-altitude mountain sheep wool with walnut and madder plant dyes.

Social & Economic Vision

By eliminating the middleman markup and presenting traditional handicrafts in high definition with rich storytelling, BalochHunar aims to increase artisan income by up to 70%, ensuring the continuation of rare handicraft techniques for future generations.
